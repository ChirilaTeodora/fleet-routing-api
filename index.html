How to Demo

  1. Click two cities to set Source & Destination.
  2. Set Fleet Size, choose Algorithm and Optimization strategy.
  3. Dispatch multiple trucks to the same destination. Watch the AI distribute routes!
  4. Click a road to simulate a blockage. Routes recalculate instantly.
  5. Use GPS Tracking to place simulated truck positions on the map.
  6. Press «ECMWF Real Weather» to fetch live European weather forecasts.
  7. Press «Compare» to show what a naive algorithm would do vs. ours.
